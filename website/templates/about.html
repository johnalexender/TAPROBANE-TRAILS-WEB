WHO WE ARE

More than a trip. A personal journey.

Serendip Private Journeys was created for travellers who want to experience Sri Lanka beyond the ordinary.

We believe the best journeys are not always the ones that follow a fixed schedule. They are the journeys where you can stop for a beautiful view, discover a quiet village, taste something local or simply spend a little longer somewhere you love.

With a private vehicle and local travel companion, you can explore Sri Lanka at your own pace while enjoying the comfort, flexibility and personal attention of a private journey.

OUR PHILOSOPHY Travel slowly. Experience deeply.
OUR STORY

Built around the traveller.

Sri Lanka is a country of incredible variety. Golden beaches, mist-covered mountains, ancient cities, wildlife, tea country and vibrant local communities can all be found within one journey.

But discovering these places is not only about reaching a destination. It is about how you get there and the experiences you have along the way.

That's why Serendip Private Journeys focuses on private travel. Instead of forcing every traveller into the same itinerary, we create journeys that can adapt to your interests, schedule and pace.
